# Third-party archival material

The MIT License covers the original spinsim implementation and project material
for which Bektur Murzaliev holds copyright. The legacy archive also preserves
older numerical routines whose source headers attribute them to other authors.
The project does not claim to relicense those contributions.

In particular, `legacy/reference/rjbesl.f` and
`legacy/reference/ribesl.f` contain Bessel and gamma routines attributed in
their own headers to W. J. Cody, L. Stoltz, David J. Sookne, and Argonne
National Laboratory. Historical paths that materialize these same bytes are
listed in `legacy/source-manifest.csv`.

These files are retained to reproduce and verify the historical implementation.
Consult their upstream provenance and applicable terms before redistributing or
using them independently.
